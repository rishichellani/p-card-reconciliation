# Acme Corp - Purchasing Card (P-Card) Spending Policy v4.2

## 1. Purpose
P-Cards exist to let employees buy low-value goods and services needed to do their job without a purchase order. Every charge must be for a legitimate business purpose, documented, and reviewed.

## 2. Eligible Expenses
2.1 Software and SaaS subscriptions used for company work. New subscriptions above $500/year require prior IT approval; the justification must say who uses the tool and for what.
2.2 Office supplies and small equipment for company use.
2.3 Business travel booked for a specific, named business trip: economy airfare, lodging up to $275/night, ground transportation, and fuel for rental cars.
2.4 Training, conferences and professional education relevant to the employee's role.
2.5 Marketing and advertising spend that belongs to an approved campaign.
2.6 Facilities maintenance and repair supplies (Facilities department).

## 3. Prohibited Expenses (never reimbursable)
3.1 Gambling and casinos.
3.2 Cash advances and ATM withdrawals.
3.3 Personal purchases of any kind, including "home office" items unless pre-approved by the employee's manager in writing (see s10).
3.4 Adult entertainment, dating and escort services.
3.5 Fines, penalties and traffic tickets.
3.6 Charges made after the cardholder's employment has ended.

## 4. Limits
4.1 Each cardholder has a single-transaction limit and a monthly limit recorded in the employee directory. These are enforced automatically.
4.2 Splitting a purchase into several smaller charges to avoid a limit is a violation, regardless of the reason given.

## 5. Meals and Entertainment
5.1 Business meals are capped at $75 per person. The justification must list every attendee (name and company) and the business topic.
5.2 Meals with no external guests and no travel or overtime context (e.g. a routine team lunch) are not reimbursable unless a manager approved them in advance.
5.3 Alcohol is allowed only when entertaining current or prospective clients, and only in moderation (no more than two drinks per person, i.e. roughly 30% of the bill).
5.4 "Team bonding", "team building" or "celebration" with no named attendees and no manager approval is not sufficient justification.

## 6. Travel
6.1 Airfare must be economy class, booked at least 7 days in advance where practical.
6.2 The justification must name the trip (destination, client or event, dates).
6.3 Personal travel, upgrades for personal benefit and companion tickets are not reimbursable.

## 7. Gifts
7.1 Gifts to clients or partners are limited to $100 per recipient per year.
7.2 The justification must name the recipient, their company, and the business relationship.
7.3 Gift cards, cash equivalents and luxury goods (jewelry, watches) are not permitted regardless of value.
7.4 Gifts to Acme employees must go through HR recognition programs, not the P-Card.

## 8. Software and Subscriptions
8.1 Only tools needed for the cardholder's work or their team's work.
8.2 Duplicate tools already licensed by the company (see IT catalog) should not be purchased separately.

## 9. Documentation
9.1 A receipt is required for every charge over $75.
9.2 Every charge needs a business justification that is specific: who, what, and why. One-word or generic answers ("supplies", "needed it", "n/a", "team stuff") are not sufficient.
9.3 Refunds and credits need no receipt but should reference the original charge.

## 10. Home Office and Personal Use
10.1 Ergonomic and home-office equipment is only covered when pre-approved by the manager and, above $200, by HR. Approval must be referenced in the justification.
10.2 Any incidental personal purchase must be reported to Finance and repaid within 30 days.

## 11. Escalation
11.1 Charges that are clearly prohibited are REJECTED.
11.2 Charges that are probably legitimate but have missing, vague or inconsistent documentation are FLAGGED for manager follow-up.
11.3 Only charges with a specific, plausible, policy-compliant justification are APPROVED.
